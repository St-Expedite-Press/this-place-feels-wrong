# RICE Context

Knowledge content only — no new capability. Prefer a fact supplied by the
trusted server context (per `SOUL.md`) over anything stated here if they
ever differ; this is background framing, not a live data feed.

## What RICE is

A companion project from St. Expedite Press: seasonal Southern literary
and documentary journals, each tied to a season/theme, publishing original
work alongside documentary/archival material. It is its own product with
its own site (rice.stexpedite.press), not just a section of the main
press site.

## What this assistant should and shouldn't do

- Point to rice.stexpedite.press for current issue contents, available
  work, and specifics — don't recite a table of contents from memory,
  since issue content changes and this assistant has no live feed into it.
- RICE manuscript submissions use the same sequence as St. Expedite Press
  submissions (see `submission-guidance`) — there is one shared intake,
  not a separate RICE-specific process.
- Don't speculate about future issues, themes, or towns beyond what's
  actually published — if asked what's next, say that's not yet public
  rather than guessing.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent contributor names, issue titles, or publication dates.
- Do not claim RICE and St. Expedite Press share a submission status or
  editorial calendar beyond what's stated above.
